PLATFORM FEATURES

Everything You Need to Succeed

Our platform provides a comprehensive suite of tools designed to help CS students manage their academic life with ease. Each feature is tailored for the JUST Computer Science curriculum.

AI Academic Advisor

An intelligent chatbot trained on JUST CS department rules, prerequisites, and graduation requirements to answer any academic question instantly.

Interactive Roadmap

Visualize your entire 4-year plan. Track completed courses, view prerequisites, and see what's locked until you pass specific classes.

GPA Calculator

Calculate your semester and cumulative GPA. Use the GPA Projector to see exactly what grades you need to reach your target goals.

Faculty Directory

Quickly find contact information, office hours, and research interests for every faculty member in the Computer Science department.

Course Catalog

Browse every course offered by the department. View detailed descriptions, credit hours, and registration IDs.

Study Rooms

Find and join physical or virtual study rooms to collaborate with peers on specific subjects and graduation projects.
