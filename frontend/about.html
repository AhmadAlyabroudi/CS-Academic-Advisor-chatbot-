Empowering Students with AI Guidance

JUST Advisor is a state-of-the-art academic platform designed specifically for Computer Science students at Jordan University of Science and Technology.

Our Mission

To simplify the academic journey by providing intelligent tools that help students plan their degree, track their progress, and interact with the department more efficiently.

Intelligent Planning

Using data-driven insights to help you navigate the complex CS curriculum and stay on track for graduation. We visualize your progress so you always know where you stand.

Student Success

Our goal is to ensure every student has the resources they need to excel academically and professionally. We bridge the gap between students and faculty.

Why JUST Advisor?

The Computer Science department at JUST is one of the most prestigious in the region. However, managing course prerequisites, tracking GPA, and finding faculty information can be overwhelming.

JUST Advisor integrates all these needs into a single, unified dashboard powered by modern AI technology. Our platform is built by students, for students, with the goal of making academic life smoother and more productive.
