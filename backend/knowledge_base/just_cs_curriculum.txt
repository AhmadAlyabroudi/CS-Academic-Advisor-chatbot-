Course: CIS221 — Fundamentals of Database Systems
Credits: 3 | Type: Faculty Compulsory Requirement
Prerequisites: CS211
Year: 2 | Semester: Spring
Description: Relational model, SQL, ER diagrams, normalization, transactions.

Course: MATH233 — Probability and Statistics
Credits: 3 | Type: Department Compulsory Requirement
Prerequisites: MATH102
Year: 2 | Semester: Spring
Description: Probability theory, distributions, statistical inference — foundational for data science and AI.
