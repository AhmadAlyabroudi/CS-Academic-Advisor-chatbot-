JUST Computer Science Department — Academic Regulations & Policies
Jordan University of Science and Technology (JUST)
====================================================================
GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS
====================================================================
- Total: 132 credit hours. University Compulsory: 16, Faculty Compulsory: 30, Department Compulsory: 74, Department Elective: 9, University Elective: 9.
- Minimum cumulative GPA (CGPA) to graduate is 2.0.

====================================================================
ACADEMIC STANDING DEFINITIONS
====================================================================
- First Year Standing: <30 credit hours completed.
- Second Year Standing: 30 to 59 credit hours completed.
- Third Year Standing: 60 to 89 credit hours completed.
- Fourth Year Standing: >=90 credit hours completed.

====================================================================
GPA GRADING SYSTEM
====================================================================
- Scale is out of 4.2 maximum:
  A+(4.2), A(4.0), A-(3.75), B+(3.5), B(3.25), B-(3.0), C+(2.75), C(2.5), C-(2.25), D+(2.0), D(1.75), D-(1.5), F(0.5).
- Retaking a course replaces the old grade in GPA calculation (both appear on transcript).

====================================================================
PREREQUISITE POLICY
====================================================================
- Must pass prerequisite with D- (1.5) or higher before registering for the next course.
- Strict system enforcement.

====================================================================
PRACTICAL TRAINING (CS391)
====================================================================
- 3 credits. Requires >=90 credit hours completed.
- Minimum 8 weeks full-time work in industry. Required report and supervisor evaluation. Done in Yr 3 Summer.

====================================================================
GRADUATION PROJECTS
====================================================================
- GP1 (CS491): 3 credits, Fall Yr 4. Requires >=90 credit hours completed. 2-4 students per team.
- GP2 (CS492): 3 credits, Spring Yr 4. Requires passing CS491. Complete implementation, report, and oral defense.

====================================================================
REGISTRATION & PROBATION POLICIES
====================================================================
- Max Credits: 18 credits in Fall/Spring, 9 credits in Summer.
- Academic Probation: Triggered when CGPA falls below 2.0. Limit of 12 credits maximum per semester. Must meet with advisor. Allowed 2 semesters to raise CGPA above 2.0 before suspension.
- Min Credits: Full-time students must register for at least 12 credits in Fall/Spring (except final semester).
- Course Withdrawal: Weeks 1-2 free; Weeks 3-10 results in "W" grade (no GPA impact); Week 11+ requires Dean approval ("WF" counts as F).

====================================================================
ELECTIVES, TRANSFERS & ADVISING
====================================================================
- Electives: 3 University Electives (9 hrs), 3 Department Electives (9 hrs). Department electives cover ML/DL, Cybersecurity, Mobile, Cloud, NLP, Vision, Advanced DB.
- Transfer Credits: Max 60 credits. Min grade C or equivalent, >=70% similarity.
- Academic Advisor: Assigned advisor helps plan registration, navigates prerequisites, signs substitutions/overloads. Advisor signature/consultation required for GP1 (CS491) and Practical Training (CS391).
