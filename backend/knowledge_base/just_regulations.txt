JUST Computer Science Department — Academic Regulations & Policies
Jordan University of Science and Technology (JUST)
Source: Official CS Department Handbook

====================================================================
GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS
====================================================================

Total Credit Hours Required: 132 credit hours
A student must successfully complete all 132 credit hours to graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from JUST.

Credit Hour Breakdown:
- University Compulsory Requirements: 16 credit hours
- Faculty Compulsory Requirements: 30 credit hours
- Department Compulsory Requirements: 74 credit hours
- Department Elective Requirements: 9 credit hours
- University Elective Requirements: 9 credit hours (3 courses × 3 credit hours each)
- Total: 132 credit hours

====================================================================
ACADEMIC STANDING DEFINITIONS
====================================================================

First Year Standing: Student has completed fewer than 30 credit hours.
Second Year Standing: Student has completed 30 to 59 credit hours.
Third Year Standing: Student has completed 60 to 89 credit hours.
Fourth Year Standing: Student has completed 90 or more credit hours.

====================================================================
GPA GRADING SYSTEM
====================================================================

JUST uses the following grade point scale (maximum GPA: 4.2):

Grade A+  = 4.2 grade points (Excellent Plus)
Grade A   = 4.0 grade points (Excellent)
Grade A-  = 3.75 grade points (Very Good Plus)
Grade B+  = 3.5 grade points (Very Good)
Grade B   = 3.25 grade points (Good Plus)
Grade B-  = 3.0 grade points (Good)
Grade C+  = 2.75 grade points (Above Average)
Grade C   = 2.5 grade points (Average)
Grade C-  = 2.25 grade points (Below Average)
Grade D+  = 2.0 grade points (Pass Plus)
Grade D   = 1.75 grade points (Pass)
Grade D-  = 1.5 grade points (Minimum Pass)
Grade F   = 0.5 grade points (Fail)

Cumulative GPA (CGPA) is calculated as: (Sum of Grade Points × Credit Hours) / (Total Credit Hours Attempted)
Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.0 to remain in good standing.

====================================================================
PREREQUISITE POLICY
====================================================================

Students must pass all listed prerequisites before registering for a course.
A grade of D- (1.5) or above is considered a passing grade for prerequisite purposes.
The prerequisite policy is strictly enforced by the registration system.

Examples of prerequisite chains:
- CS101 → SE112 → CS216 → SE230 → SE320
- CS101 → CS211 → CS284 → CS375 (Operating Systems)
- CS211 → CIS221 (Database Systems)
- MATH241 + CS101 → CS282 (Theory of Computing)
- CS284 + CPE252 → CS375 (Operating Systems)
- CS375 + CS451 → CS475 (Distributed Systems)

====================================================================
PRACTICAL TRAINING (CS391) REQUIREMENTS
====================================================================

Practical Training (CS391) is a mandatory 3-credit-hour course.
Eligibility: Students must have successfully completed at least 90 credit hours before registering.
Duration: Minimum of 8 weeks of full-time work in a relevant industry organization.
Deliverables: Student must submit a training report and receive a supervisor evaluation.
Timing: Typically completed during the Summer of Year 3.

====================================================================
GRADUATION PROJECT REQUIREMENTS
====================================================================

The Graduation Project is split across two semesters:

Graduation Project 1 (CS491) — Fall Semester, Year 4:
- Prerequisites: Must have passed at least 90 credit hours.
- Credits: 3 credit hours.
- Requirements: Topic proposal, literature review, supervisor assignment, and initial implementation.
- Team size: 2 to 4 students per project team.
- Supervisor: Each team must have an assigned faculty supervisor.

Graduation Project 2 (CS492) — Spring Semester, Year 4:
- Prerequisites: Must have passed CS491 (Graduation Project 1).
- Credits: 3 credit hours.
- Requirements: Full system implementation, testing, final report, and public presentation/defense before a jury.
- Jury evaluation: Final grade includes jury assessment during the defense presentation.

====================================================================
COURSE REGISTRATION POLICIES
====================================================================

Maximum Credit Hours per Semester:
- Regular semester (Fall/Spring): Maximum of 18 credit hours.
- Summer semester: Maximum of 9 credit hours.
- Students on academic probation are limited to 12 credit hours per semester.

Minimum Credit Hours per Semester:
- Full-time students must register for at least 12 credit hours per regular semester.
- Exception: Final semester students may register for fewer if only a small number of credits remain.

Course Withdrawal Policy:
- Students may withdraw from a course within the first two weeks without any academic penalty.
- Late withdrawal (weeks 3–10) results in a "W" grade on the transcript (not counted in GPA).
- Withdrawal after week 10 requires dean's approval and results in a "WF" (counted as F in GPA).

====================================================================
ACADEMIC PROBATION
====================================================================

A student is placed on Academic Probation if their CGPA falls below 2.0.
Students on probation:
- Are limited to 12 credit hours per semester.
- Must meet with their academic advisor each semester.
- Have 2 semesters to raise their CGPA above 2.0, or they face suspension.

Academic suspension means the student cannot register for one full academic year.
After suspension, students may return but remain on probation until CGPA exceeds 2.0.

====================================================================
ELECTIVE COURSES POLICY
====================================================================

University Elective Requirements: Students must complete 3 university elective courses (9 credit hours total) from the list approved by the university.

Department Elective Requirements: Students must complete 3 department elective courses (9 credit hours total) from the CS department-approved elective list.
These courses cover advanced topics such as:
- Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Cybersecurity
- Mobile Application Development
- Cloud Computing
- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
- Advanced Database Systems

====================================================================
TRANSFER CREDIT POLICY
====================================================================

Students transferring from other universities or faculties may apply for course equivalency.
Maximum transfer credits accepted: 60 credit hours.
Transferred courses must be equivalent in content (minimum 70% similarity) and grade (minimum C or equivalent).
Transfer requests must be submitted within the first semester of enrollment.

====================================================================
ACADEMIC ADVISOR ASSIGNMENT
====================================================================

Each student is assigned a faculty academic advisor from the CS department.
The academic advisor helps students:
- Plan their course registration each semester.
- Navigate prerequisite requirements.
- Address academic difficulties.
- Approve course substitutions or overloads.
Students must consult their academic advisor before registering for Graduation Project (CS491) and Practical Training (CS391).

====================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
====================================================================

Q: What is the minimum GPA to graduate?
A: Students need a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 to graduate. Some honors recognitions require higher GPAs.

Q: Can I take courses from other departments?
A: Yes, for university and department elective slots, students may take approved courses from other departments with advisor approval.

Q: How long does it typically take to complete the CS degree?
A: The standard completion time is 4 years (8 regular semesters + 1 summer for practical training).

Q: What happens if I fail a compulsory course?
A: You must retake the course. The new grade replaces the failing grade in the GPA calculation, but both attempts appear on the transcript.

Q: Can I register for CS491 (GP1) without finishing all my courses?
A: No. You must have completed at least 90 credit hours to register for CS491 or CS391.

Q: What is the prerequisite for CS284 (Algorithms)?
A: CS284 requires CS211 (Data Structures). To take CS211, you need to have passed both MATH241 and CS101.

Q: What is the prerequisite for CS342 (Computer Networks)?
A: CS342 requires CS284 (Analysis and Design of Algorithms).

Q: What is the prerequisite for CS375 (Operating Systems)?
A: CS375 requires both CS284 (Algorithms) and CPE252 (Computer Organization and Design).

Q: What is the prerequisite for CS362 (Artificial Intelligence)?
A: CS362 requires CS284 (Analysis and Design of Algorithms).

Q: What are the prerequisites for CY261 (Cryptography)?
A: CY261 requires SE112, SE113 (OOP + Lab), and MATH233 (Probability and Statistics).

Q: How is my GPA calculated at JUST?
A: CGPA = Sum of (Grade Points × Credit Hours for each course) divided by Total Credit Hours attempted. The maximum possible GPA at JUST is 4.2 (for A+).

Q: Can I improve a grade by retaking a course?
A: Yes. If you retake a course, the higher grade is used in GPA calculations, but all attempts are shown on the transcript.

Q: What is the role of the practical training course?
A: CS391 (Practical Training) provides real-world industry experience. It must be completed after earning 90 credit hours and typically takes place during the summer after Year 3. You must secure a position at an approved company and complete a minimum of 8 weeks of full-time work.
